Privacy Policy for 15-Minute Reminder

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the 15-Minute Reminder Alexa skill ("the Skill") handles information when you use it on Amazon Alexa-enabled devices.

1. Information We Access and Use

  • Alexa Reminders Permission: With your explicit permission, the Skill uses the Amazon Alexa Reminders API solely to schedule your requested 15-, 10-, and 5-minute countdown reminders on your Alexa account.
  • Voice Inputs (End Time): When you speak a target end time, Amazon Alexa processes the audio and passes only the parsed time value to the Skill's backend function to calculate and schedule your reminders.

2. Data Storage and Retention

  • No External Storage: The Skill does not store, log, or retain your voice recordings, reminder text, schedule times, name, email address, or Amazon account details in any external database.
  • All scheduled reminders remain stored exclusively within your personal Amazon Alexa account, where you can view, edit, or delete them at any time via the Alexa app or by voice.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.
  • Purchase and entitlement verification for this paid skill is handled securely by Amazon.

4. Children's Privacy

The Skill is designed to be configured and operated by parents and caregivers to manage household screen-time transitions. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Revoking Permissions and Deleting Data

You can revoke the Skill's access to Alexa Reminders at any time in the Alexa mobile app under More > Skills & Games > Your Skills > 15-Minute Reminder > Settings > Manage Permissions, or by disabling the Skill.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on the Amazon Alexa Skill Store page.
